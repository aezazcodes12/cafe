How It Began

From Micro-Batches to Neighborhood Benchmark

Brew & Bean began when our founders traveled through the cloud-forest coffee estates of Huila, Colombia. Struck by the profound connection between soil, climate, and bean flavor, they pledged to build a café where every cup honors the growers behind the crop.

Today, our café retains that same micro-roastery spirit. We roast in modest 12-kilogram batches, recording temperature curves down to the second so each origin shines with unmistakable clarity.

The Bean-to-Cup Process

A behind-the-scenes journey of how our green coffee cherries transform into your morning cup.

Step 01

Ethical Sourcing

Hand-harvested ripe red cherries sourced from certified high-altitude organic farms across Africa and the Americas.

Step 02

Master Roasting

Small batches gently rotated in our cast-iron drum roaster to unlock honey sweetness and intricate flavor notes.

Step 03

Precision Grind

Micron-accurate flat burr grinders dialed in fresh every morning to maximize flavor extraction efficiency.

Step 04

Barista Extraction

Brewed at 9 bars of pressure at 93°C, finished with silky microfoam poured with signature barista latte art.
