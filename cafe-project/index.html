The Brew & Bean Promise

Crafted with Purpose, Served with Love

We believe a café should be more than a morning caffeine stop—it is a sanctuary of comfort, community, and unforgettable flavor.

Direct-Trade Beans

We source directly from high-altitude family farms in Colombia, Ethiopia, and Guatemala, ensuring ethical living wages.

Small-Batch Roasting

Every bean is roasted on our custom cast-iron roaster to bring out subtle caramel, floral, and dark cacao notes.

Scratch Bakery

Our bakers arrive before sunrise to roll 72-layer croissants, bake Basque cheesecakes, and knead organic sourdough.

Cozy Workspace

Our Heritage

Born from a Simple Passion for Exceptional Coffee

Brew & Bean began as a humble roastery workshop in 2014. What started as three friends tasting micro-lots on a kitchen table has blossomed into a cherished community gathering space.

Every morning, our master roaster listens to the first crack of beans in our drum roaster, ensuring the perfect harmony of sweetness, acidity, and aromatic depth.
